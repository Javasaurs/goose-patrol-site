Design process and evolution

Diagrams are written in PlantUML (UML 2.5) and versioned in Git. The architecture evolved in three versions:

  • v1 – template. gdx-liftoff generated the two-module layout (lwjgl3 + core): the Platform and Game layers.
  • v2 – map and player. Added the Tiled campus map and PlayerController.
  • v3 – geese (current). Added GooseController and the BaseGoose hierarchy (Duck, Goose, Swan).
